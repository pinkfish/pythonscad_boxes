MarchOfTheAnts (march_of_the_ants)
==================================

March of the Ants board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/march_of_the_ants/march_of_the_ants.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``195 × 275 × 65 mm`` (usable height ``59 mm`` with ``6 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 10 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
