RussianRailroads (russian_railroads)
====================================

Russian Railroads board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/russian_railroads/russian_railroads.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``219 × 308 × 70 mm`` (usable height ``55 mm`` with ``15 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 10 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
