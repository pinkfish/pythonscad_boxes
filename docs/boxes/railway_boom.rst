RailwayBoom (railway_boom)
==========================

Railway Boom board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/railway_boom/railway_boom.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``209 × 300 × 46 mm`` (usable height ``28 mm`` with ``18 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 9 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
