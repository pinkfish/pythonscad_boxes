BucketKing (bucket_king)
========================

Bucket King 3D board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/bucket_king/bucket_king.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``140 × 288 × 70 mm``
- **Boxes**: 7 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
