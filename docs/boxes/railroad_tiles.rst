RailroadTiles (railroad_tiles)
==============================

Railroad Tiles board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/railroad_tiles/railroad_tiles.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``230 × 230 × 63 mm`` (usable height ``60.5 mm`` with ``2.5 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 15 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
