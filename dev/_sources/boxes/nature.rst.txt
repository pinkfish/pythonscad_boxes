.. _box-insert-nature:

Nature (nature)
===============

Nature board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/nature/nature.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``210 × 210 × 75 mm`` (usable height ``68 mm`` with ``7 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 7 sub-box(es), 4 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
