.. _box-insert-1835:

EighteenThirtyFive (1835)
=========================

1835 board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/1835/1835.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``216 × 298 × 50 mm`` (usable height ``35 mm`` with ``15 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 9 sub-box(es), 2 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
