ExplorersOfNavoriaForgottenLands (explorers_of_navoria_forgotten_lands)
=======================================================================

Explorers of Navoria: Forgotten Lands expansion organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/explorers_of_navoria_forgotten_lands/explorers_of_navoria_forgotten_lands.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``211 × 268 × 50 mm`` (usable height ``46 mm`` with ``4 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 5 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
