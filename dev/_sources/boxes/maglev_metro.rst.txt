MaglevMetro (maglev_metro)
==========================

Maglev Metro board game organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/maglev_metro/maglev_metro.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``286 × 286 × 65 mm`` (usable height ``47.8 mm`` with ``17.2 mm`` board storage)
- **Boxes**: 14 sub-box(es), 3 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
