EarthAnimalKingdom (earth_animal_kingdom)
=========================================

Earth: Animal Kingdom expansion organizer.

- **Source Script**: ``boxes/earth_animal_kingdom/earth_animal_kingdom.py``
- **Game Box Dimensions**: ``288 × 158 × 47 mm``
- **Boxes**: 5 sub-box(es), 2 spacer tray(s)
- **Box Types Used**: :ref:`BoxType.SLIDING <box-type-sliding>`, :ref:`BoxType.CAP <box-type-cap>`, :ref:`BoxType.NO_LID <box-type-no-lid>`
