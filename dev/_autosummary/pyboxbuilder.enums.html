    class pyboxbuilder.enums.FingerCut(*values)[source]¶

    Bases: Enum

    How a compartment’s finger cutout gets the contents out (FR-060).

    The two answer different situations, and which one a well wants is about what is in it rather than how big it is:

    THROUGH_FLOOR

    A hole through the box’s base at one wall, so a thumb pushes the contents up from underneath. What a stack needs — a card stack fills its well, leaving no side for a finger to reach down. The original toolkit cuts every card box this way.

    SCOOP

    A dip in the side of the well, for loose pieces a finger goes in beside: tokens, a bag, a board. It leaves the base solid.

    class pyboxbuilder.enums.PatternType(*values)[source]¶

    Bases: Enum

    Lid through-hole pattern (FR-023).

    Every member here is a shape the library actually draws. It listed 47 and drew three: the tessellations and pentagon tilings wrapped an import of a package this repo does not contain, inside a bare except, and fell back to square holes — so a lid asking for LEAF got squares and nothing said so. A member is added when the geometry to draw it is (FR-000c).

    CIRCLE = 'circle'¶

    Round holes on a square grid.

    DENSE_HEX = 'dense_hex'¶

    The honeycomb at a tighter pitch.

    DENSE_TRIANGLE = 'dense_triangle'¶

    The triangle grid at a tighter pitch.

    HEX = 'hex'¶

    Hexagonal holes in staggered rows — a honeycomb.

    NONE = 'none'¶

    No pattern — a solid lid.

    OCTAGON = 'octagon'¶

    Octagonal holes, leaving small square webs between them.

    SQUARE = 'square'¶

    Square holes on a square grid.

    TRIANGLE = 'triangle'¶

    Triangular holes, alternating point-up and point-down.

    VORONOI = 'voronoi'¶

    Round holes of varying size on a jittered grid — an organic scatter.

    class pyboxbuilder.enums.StackableMode(*values)[source]¶

    Bases: Enum

    How a no-lid box’s interlocking rim mates with its neighbour (FR-038).

    Both modes stack securely; the choice decides whether the rim sits inside the box above or wraps around the box below.

    INSIDE = 'inside'¶

    A recess in the top rim that the box above nests down into.

    OUTSIDE = 'outside'¶

    A ridge around the outside that fits over the box below.
