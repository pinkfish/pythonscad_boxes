    GulfMobileAndOhio (gulf_mobile_and_ohio)¶

    Gulf, Mobile & Ohio board game organizer.

    • Source Script: boxes/gulf_mobile_and_ohio/gulf_mobile_and_ohio.py

    • Game Box Dimensions: 217 × 307 × 39 mm (usable height 29.5 mm with 9.5 mm board storage)

    • Boxes: 10 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)

    • Box Types Used: BoxType.SLIDING, BoxType.CAP, BoxType.NO_LID
