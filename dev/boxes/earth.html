    Earth (earth)¶

    Earth (+ Abundance expansion) board game organizer.

    • Source Script: boxes/earth/earth.py

    • Game Box Dimensions: 288 × 288 × 72 mm (usable height 55.2 mm with 16.8 mm board storage)

    • Boxes: 26 sub-box(es), 1 spacer tray(s)

    • Box Types Used: BoxType.SLIDING, BoxType.FILAMENT_HINGE, BoxType.SLIPOVER, BoxType.NO_LID, BoxType.CAP
