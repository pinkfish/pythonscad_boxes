    TheCrewDeepSeaSleeved (the_crew_deep_sea_sleeved)¶

    The Crew: Mission Deep Sea — box insert for sleeved cards.

    • Source Script: boxes/the_crew_deep_sea_sleeved/the_crew_deep_sea_sleeved.py

    • Game Box Dimensions: 172 × 122 × 36 mm

    • Boxes: 4 sub-box(es), 3 spacer tray(s)

    • Box Types Used: BoxType.SLIDING, BoxType.NO_LID
