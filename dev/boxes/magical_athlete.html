    MagicalAthlete (magical_athlete)¶

    Magical Athlete board game organizer.

    • Source Script: boxes/magical_athlete/magical_athlete.py

    • Game Box Dimensions: 184 × 294 × 45 mm (usable height 38 mm with 7 mm board storage)

    • Boxes: 6 sub-box(es), 2 spacer tray(s)

    • Box Types Used: BoxType.SLIDING, BoxType.CAP, BoxType.NO_LID
