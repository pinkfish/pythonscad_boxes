    Railways of the World (railways_of_the_world)¶

    Railways of the World board game insert.

    • Source Script: boxes/railways_of_the_world/railways_of_the_world.py

    • Game Box Dimensions: 310 × 385 × 100 mm (usable height 78 mm with 22 mm board storage)

    • Boxes: 25 sub-box(es), 2 spacer tray(s)

    • Box Types Used: BoxType.SLIDING, BoxType.CAP, BoxType.NO_LID
